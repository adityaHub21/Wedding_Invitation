PUBLISH FOR A PUBLIC LINK (Netlify Drop):

3. Extract this ZIP first.
4. Drag the extracted folder containing index.html onto the Netlify Drop page (or follow its upload instructions).
5. Netlify will provide a public URL. Open that URL on your phone and share it in WhatsApp groups.
